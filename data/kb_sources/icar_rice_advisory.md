# Paddy (Rice) Stem Borer and Leaf Folder Advisory
Source: ICAR-National Rice Research Institute (NRRI), Cuttack
Region: All India
Crop: Rice
Season: Kharif
Publication Date: 2026-07-05

## Yellow Stem Borer Control
Yellow stem borer causes dead hearts in young rice plants and white ears during panicle emergence.
- Avoid excessive nitrogen fertilizer application.
- Clip leaf tips of seedlings before transplanting to remove egg masses.
- Install pheromone traps at 5 per hectare for monitoring.
- If dead hearts exceed 5 percent, apply Cartap Hydrochloride 4G granules at 10 kg per acre in standing water or spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC at 0.3 ml per liter of water as advised by local KVK.
