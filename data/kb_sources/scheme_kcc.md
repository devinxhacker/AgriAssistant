# Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme Overview
Source: NABARD & Ministry of Agriculture (KCC Circular)
Region: All India
Crop: All
Season: All
Publication Date: 2026-06-15

## Purpose and Interest Subvention
- Kisan Credit Card provides timely, affordable short-term credit to farmers for crop cultivation, post-harvest expenses, and working capital for allied activities such as dairy and fisheries.
- Short-term crop loans up to Rs 3 lakh are eligible for interest subvention, making the effective interest rate 7 percent per annum, and 4 percent per annum for farmers who repay promptly within one year (3 percent prompt repayment incentive).
- Collateral-free loan limit under KCC is up to Rs 1.60 lakh (and up to Rs 3 lakh for tie-up arrangements).
- Farmers can apply through any commercial bank, Regional Rural Bank (RRB), cooperative bank, or via CSC with Aadhaar, land records, and passport-size photograph.
