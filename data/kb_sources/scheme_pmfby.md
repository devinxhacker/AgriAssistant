# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) Farmer Guide
Source: Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (PMFBY Operational Guidelines)
Region: All India
Crop: All
Season: Kharif and Rabi
Publication Date: 2026-07-01

## Premium Rates and Coverage
- Farmers pay a maximum uniform premium of 2 percent of the sum insured for Kharif crops, 1.5 percent for Rabi crops, and 5 percent for commercial and horticultural crops. The remaining premium is subsidized by the Central and State Governments.
- Risks covered: Prevented sowing due to deficit rainfall, standing crop losses due to drought, flood, hailstorm, pests and diseases, and post-harvest losses up to 14 days after harvesting for crops kept in cut-and-spread condition.

## How to Report Crop Loss Within 72 Hours
- In case of localized calamities (hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloudburst) or post-harvest loss, the insured farmer must report the loss within 72 hours.
- Farmers can report via the Krishi Rakshak Portal and Helpline 14447, the Crop Insurance mobile app, or by informing their bank branch or agriculture department officer.
- Enrollment is voluntary for both loanee and non-loanee farmers through CSCs, banks, or pmfby.gov.in before the seasonal cut-off date (typically July 31 for Kharif and December 31 for Rabi).
