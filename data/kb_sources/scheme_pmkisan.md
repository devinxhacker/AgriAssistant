# PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi) Guidelines
Source: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare (PM-KISAN Portal)
Region: All India
Crop: All
Season: All
Publication Date: 2026-08-01

## Benefit and Eligibility
- Under PM-KISAN, eligible landholding farmer families receive Rs 6,000 per year in three equal installments of Rs 2,000 every four months directly into their Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts.
- Eligibility: All cultivable landholding farmer families subject to exclusion criteria (institutional landholders, constitutional post holders, serving/retired government employees, and income tax payers are excluded).

## Mandatory Requirements for Receiving Installments
1. e-KYC is mandatory for all registered PM-KISAN farmers. Farmers can complete OTP-based e-KYC on pmkisan.gov.in or biometric e-KYC at their nearest Common Service Centre (CSC).
2. Aadhaar seeding with the bank account (DBT enabled) and land record seeding (Bhu-lekh verification) must be completed.
3. Farmers can check their beneficiary status and installment history on pmkisan.gov.in using their registration number or mobile number, or call the PM-KISAN helpline 155261 / 011-24300606.
