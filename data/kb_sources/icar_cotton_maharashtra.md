# Cotton Crop Advisory: Yellowing Leaves and Pink Bollworm Management
Source: ICAR-Central Institute for Cotton Research (CICR), Nagpur
Region: Maharashtra
Crop: Cotton
Season: Kharif
Publication Date: 2026-07-15

## Yellowing of Cotton Leaves (Parawilt / Nitrogen Deficiency)
Yellowing of leaves in cotton during active vegetative or flowering stage is commonly caused by waterlogging after heavy rain, nitrogen deficiency, or magnesium deficiency.
- First, drain excess standing water from the field immediately.
- If yellowing is due to nitrogen deficiency after heavy rain, apply 2 percent urea foliar spray (20 grams of urea per liter of water) or top-dress recommended nitrogen once soil moisture is normal.
- For magnesium deficiency (reddish-purple or interveinal yellowing on older leaves), spray Magnesium Sulphate at 10 grams per liter of water.
- Avoid spraying any chemical mixture during hot afternoon hours; spray in the morning or evening.

## Pink Bollworm (Gulabi Bond Ali / Gulabi Sundi) Control
Pink bollworm larvae bore into cotton bolls and damage lint and seeds.
- Install pheromone traps at 5 traps per hectare starting 45 days after sowing to monitor moth activity.
- If trap catches exceed 8 moths per trap for 3 consecutive nights, or rosette flowers exceed 10 percent, take control measures.
- Spray Neem oil 1500 ppm at 5 ml per liter of water as an early preventive measure.
- For severe infestation above economic threshold level, spray Profenofos 50 EC at 2 ml per liter of water or Emamectin Benzoate 5 SG at 0.4 grams per liter of water as per KVK recommendation.
- Do not extend the crop beyond 180 days; terminate crop on time to break the pest cycle.
