# Wheat Yellow Rust and Nutrient Advisory
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Wheat and Barley Research (IIWBR), Karnal
Region: Uttar Pradesh
Crop: Wheat
Season: Rabi
Publication Date: 2026-10-10

## Yellow Rust (Pila Ratua) Identification and Control
Yellow rust appears as yellow pustules arranged in narrow stripes on wheat leaves, especially during cool humid weather in December to February.
- Grow resistant varieties such as DBW 187, DBW 222, or HD 3226.
- Inspect fields weekly in early morning; if yellow powder rubs off on fingers from striped leaves, it is yellow rust.
- Immediately spray Propiconazole 25 EC at 1 ml per liter of water (200 ml in 200 liters of water per acre) on the affected patch and surrounding border.
- Repeat spray after 15 days only if fresh pustules appear, and consult your local KVK for confirmation.

## Irrigation and Nitrogen Top Dressing
- First irrigation at Crown Root Initiation (CRI) stage, 21 days after sowing, is critical.
- Split nitrogen application: apply half at sowing and remaining half with first and second irrigation using urea.
