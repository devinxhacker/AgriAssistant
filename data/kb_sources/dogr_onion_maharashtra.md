# Onion Thrips and Storage Advisory
Source: ICAR-Directorate of Onion and Garlic Research (DOGR), Rajgurunagar
Region: Maharashtra
Crop: Onion
Season: Rabi
Publication Date: 2026-09-12

## Onion Thrips Management
Thrips cause silvery-white blotches and curling on onion leaves, reducing bulb size.
- Plant barrier crops like 2 rows of maize around the onion plot to reduce thrips migration.
- Spray Neem oil 1500 ppm at 3 ml per liter of water at early stage.
- If thrips exceed 30 per plant, spray Fipronil 5 SC at 1.5 ml per liter of water or Spinosad 45 SC at 0.3 ml per liter of water with a sticker.

## Post-Harvest Curing and Storage
- Stop irrigation 15 days before harvesting to improve bulb shelf life.
- Cure harvested onion bulbs in shade for 10 to 15 days until necks are completely dry before storing in ventilated chawls.
