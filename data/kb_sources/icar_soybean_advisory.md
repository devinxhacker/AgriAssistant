# Soybean Cultivation and Pest Advisory
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Soybean Research (IISR), Indore & VNMKV Parbhani
Region: Maharashtra
Crop: Soybean
Season: Kharif
Publication Date: 2026-06-20

## Sowing and Nutrient Management
- Recommended varieties for Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh include JS 95-60, MAUS 158, and Phule Sangam (KDS 726).
- Sow at 45 cm row-to-row and 5 cm plant-to-plant spacing after receiving 75 to 100 mm monsoon rainfall.
- Seed treatment: Treat seed with Rhizobium and PSB biofertilizer at 5 grams per kg seed before sowing.

## Girdle Beetle and Semilooper Management
- Monitor fields regularly from 20 days after sowing for girdle beetle rings on stems and green semilooper larvae on leaves.
- Erect bird perches at 10 to 15 per hectare for natural predation.
- If leaf damage exceeds 20 percent or 3 to 4 larvae are seen per meter row, spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC at 0.3 ml per liter of water (150 ml per hectare in 500 liters of water).
- Always wear gloves and mask while handling insecticides and keep away from water sources.
